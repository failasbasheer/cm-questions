Community Medicine Recall

Park's Textbook of Preventive and Social Medicine, 23rd edition. Built from the book's own pages; every point shows its Park page number.

How to use this
  1. Read the question. Close the book. Write the headings you remember on rough paper.
  2. Tap Show skeleton and compare. Circle what you missed.
  3. Open Park at the page shown and read only what you missed.
  4. Mark ✓ or ✗. Next round, tap the ✗ only filter and redo just those.

Memory hooks are in English + Malayalam; tap Hooks: English only at the top to switch. Lines in orange marked ≈ are not in Park (the syllabus asks for them). They're standard content written to fill the gap.

Writing a 15-mark essay
  1. Definition in the book's words (2–3 lines).
  2. Headings straight from the skeleton, underlined.
  3. Under each heading: 3–5 points, with numbers (%, years, Acts).
  4. One diagram or table where it says "Draw".
  5. Conclusion (1–2 lines): why it matters to the physiotherapist / community.
